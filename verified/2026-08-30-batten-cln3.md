# Batten / CLN3 retina report — VERIFIED

Status: verified entry (source held)
Date: 2026-08-30 (verified 2026-08-28, posted same day)

## Report
Juvenile Batten disease (CLN3) announces itself as vision loss. A 3D human
pluripotent stem cell retina model shows the damage starts in the support
layer (retinal pigment epithelium, RPE), not the photoreceptors: the
CLN3Δex7-8 mutation cuts acid ceramidase. Rescue worked in organoids and
miniswine.

## Source
Han et al., Science Translational Medicine, doi:10.1126/scitranslmed.ady7616

## Frame caveat (kept with the claim)
RPE involvement was hypothesized as early as 2021 (Commun Biol 4:864).
The model strengthens the mechanism; it did not invent the hypothesis.
