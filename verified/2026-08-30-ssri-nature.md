## Report
The Nature news feature on SSRI prescribing: 100M+ prescriptions filled in
2024 (sertraline, escitalopram, fluoxetine); roughly 1 in 6 US adults takes
an antidepressant. Effect size context: Stone BMJ 2022 meta-analysis, 200+
trials, ~10 vs ~7 points. The political claim that SSRIs are "on a par with
heroin" is the overstatement the feature corrects. Withdrawal is real and
serious (Horowitz/Fava literature), which is not the same as equivalence.
